{% section "Overview", "overview" %}

<p class="note"><strong>Estimated time:</strong> Approximately 3–4 hours</p>

Producing an analysis is only one part of effective data practice. Your findings also need to be communicated in ways that help other people understand the evidence, make decisions, and take appropriate action.

In this module, you will extend the Share stage of the course progression by learning how to communicate data findings through visualizations, summaries, dashboards, presentations, and stakeholder-facing narratives. You will explore how AI can help you create and refine these products while learning to evaluate whether the resulting communication is accurate, clear, accessible, and appropriate for its intended audience.

You will also examine your responsibilities when using AI in data work. Issues such as accountability, privacy, bias, authenticity, intellectual property, regulation, and environmental impact become increasingly important as AI is integrated into analytical workflows and organizational decision-making.

Finally, you will look ahead through the perspectives of practicing data professionals. The industry practitioners whose work informed the AI-Enhanced Data Practitioner profile also discussed the trends they believe are reshaping data work, including agentic AI, context engineering, increased automation, changing roles, and growing expectations for accountability. The particular tools you use will continue to change, but foundational data knowledge, organizational understanding, critical thinking, communication, responsible judgment, and continuous learning will remain central to your work as an AI-Enhanced Data Practitioner.

{% section "Essential Question and Objectives", "essential-question-and-objectives" %}

### Essential Question

**How can you communicate AI-supported data insights clearly and responsibly while adapting to a changing world of data work?**

### By the End of This Module, You Will Be Able To:

- Communicate data findings clearly and appropriately to technical and nontechnical audiences.
- Use AI to help create and refine visualizations, summaries, dashboards, and stakeholder-facing narratives.
- Evaluate AI-supported communications for accuracy, clarity, context, accessibility, and potential misrepresentation.
- Identify responsible AI concerns related to accountability, privacy, bias, authenticity, intellectual property, regulation, and environmental impacts.
- Explain how developments such as agentic AI, context engineering, and increased automation may change data work.
- Describe why data architecture, documentation, evaluation, and human oversight become increasingly important as AI automates more of the analytical process.
- Identify areas in which you will need to continuously develop your knowledge and skills as an AI-Enhanced Data Practitioner.
