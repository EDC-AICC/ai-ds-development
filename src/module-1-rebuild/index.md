{% section "This module", "this-module" %}

AI can now write code, clean data, and draft analyses in seconds. This module is about what that changes in a data practitioner's work, and what stays with you.

There are four parts, and each one works the same way. You start with a piece of AI-generated work and something to check or work out. Then you read what happened and why.

1. **The Evolving Role.** An AI writes a script in thirty seconds. You find out what it couldn't have known.
2. **Context Is King.** Calculations that are correct and still mean the wrong thing, in a hospital, a call center, and a hardware chain.
3. **When AI Fails.** Three ways AI-generated work goes wrong without an error message.
4. **Decision Lab.** You audit an AI's recommendation yourself and decide what to do with it.

Keep one idea in mind throughout the module.

{% callout "highlight" %}
AI can accelerate data work, but you remain responsible for the quality, context, and consequences of the work.
{% endcallout %}

{% endreflect %}

{% section "The role", "the-role" %}

This course is built on an industry-informed profile of a role called the AI-Enhanced Data Practitioner. The profile defines the role this way.

> **The AI-Enhanced Data Practitioner, within an organizational context, leverages, orchestrates, assesses, and innovates with AI technology and systems to accelerate and more effectively support the data lifecycle and data governance to collect, transform, and analyze data, build in-house agentic AI tools and fine-tune models, evaluate and communicate results in order to inform and guide decision-making and produce products/services and increase efficiencies.**

The course looks at different parts of this definition. This module starts with one of them: knowing what AI can speed up, and what you still need to understand, check, and decide.

{% section "Essential Question and Objectives", "essential-question-and-objectives" %}

### Essential Question

**How does AI change your work as a data practitioner, and what knowledge and judgment must you continue to provide?**

### By the End of This Module, You Will Be Able To:

- Describe the role and responsibilities of the AI-Enhanced Data Practitioner.
- Explain how AI is changing common data science tasks and workflows.
- Identify ways AI can support different stages of the data lifecycle.
- Explain why organizational, business, stakeholder, and user context matters in data work.
- Identify tasks and situations in which AI can appropriately support your work.
- Recognize situations requiring significant human expertise, judgment, review, or intervention.
- Explain why AI-generated data, code, analyses, and recommendations must be critically evaluated.
- Identify common risks and failure modes in AI-generated data work.
