Module 3 · Redesign

Four layout concepts

The same Part 1 content, organized four different ways. All use the site's existing palette, type, and dark mode — only the structure changes.

Concept 1

Stages

The part becomes five focused screens with a numbered rail across the top. One thing on screen at a time; no long scroll anywhere.

Concept 2

Course shell

A persistent sidebar maps the whole module — every part, every section — beside the lesson. The page scrolls, but you always know where you are.

Concept 3

Hub and spokes

Each part is a short hub: the story plus a card per piece of work, with progress tracking. Each card opens a focused screen holding exactly one thing.

Concept 4

The desk

Reading on the left, the material — email, activity, checklist, video — pinned on the right while you read about it. Folds to one column on mobile.
